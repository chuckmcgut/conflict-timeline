# Conflict Timeline

Self-contained interactive history atlases: an animated map plus a timeline with one lane per theater, listed here in chronological order. Each is a single HTML file; open it in a browser (map libraries load from a CDN).

- `caribbean-atlas.html` — The Caribbean: settlement, European contact, conquest, slavery and revolt, wars, treaties and independence, 6000 BC–2024
- `greek-wars-atlas.html` — Persian and Peloponnesian Wars, 499–404 BC
- `alexander-the-great-atlas.html` — Alexander's campaigns, 336–323 BC
- `punic-wars-atlas.html` — Punic Wars, 264–146 BC
- `marius-and-sulla-atlas.html` — Marius, Sulla and the first Roman civil war: the Jugurthine, Cimbrian, Social and Mithridatic wars, 112–77 BC
- `gallic-and-roman-civil-wars-atlas.html` — Caesar's Gallic War and the Roman civil wars, 58–30 BC
- `crusades-atlas.html` — Crusades, 1095–1291
- `mongol-conquests-atlas.html` — Mongol conquests, 1206–1294
- `hundred-years-war-atlas.html` — Hundred Years' War, 1337–1453
- `roses-and-civil-war-atlas.html` — Wars of the Roses and the English Civil War, 1450–1660
- `spanish-conquest-atlas.html` — Spanish conquest of the Americas: the Caribbean, the Aztec, Maya and Inca empires, the Muisca and Mapuche, and the entradas into North America, 1492–1600
- `american-indian-wars-atlas.html` — American Indian Wars, 1600–1890
- `thirty-years-war-atlas.html` — Thirty Years' War, from the Defenestration of Prague to the Peace of Westphalia, 1618–1648
- `minnesota-history-atlas.html` — Minnesota history: treaties and conflicts with the Dakota and Ojibwe, mining, milling and railroads, 1660–2021
- `seven-years-war-atlas.html` — Seven Years' War in Europe, North America, the Caribbean, Africa and India, 1754–1763
- `revolutionary-war-atlas.html` — American Revolutionary War, 1773–1783
- `napoleonic-wars-atlas.html` — Napoleonic Wars, 1789–1815
- `mexican-american-war-atlas.html` — Texas Revolution and Mexican–American War, 1835–1854
- `crimean-war-atlas.html` — Crimean War, 1853–1856
- `civil-war-atlas.html` — American Civil War, 1860–1865
- `wwi-atlas.html` — World War I, 1914–1918
- `russian-civil-war-atlas.html` — Russian Revolution and Civil War, 1917–1922
- `china-wars-atlas.html` — Chinese Civil War and Second Sino-Japanese War, 1927–1950
- `wwii-atlas.html` — World War II, 1939–1945
- `middle-east-conflicts-atlas.html` — Israel–Palestine, Yemen and Iran, 1947–2026
- `vietnam-war-atlas.html` — Vietnam War, 1954–1976

Maps use present-day borders. Events, dates and casualty figures are from general knowledge (the Middle East entries for 2025–26 were checked against public web sources) and many figures are disputed or rounded.
